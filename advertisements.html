The No-Loss Guarantee

Underperform in Month One? Month Two Is Free.

Every new campaign gets a 30-day trial run. If it doesn't hit the minimum tracked leads or tracked clicks for your spend level — numbers straight from your own ad account — your second month of management is on us. No cash changes hands, no back-and-forth about what counts, just an extra month to get it right.

Your ad spend goes straight to Meta or Google, so that part was never ours to touch — but the fee you pay us to manage it? If month one underperforms, month two costs you nothing.

There's really no reason not to try it.

Guarantee is satisfied if your campaign reaches either the clicks or leads threshold for your spend level, whichever comes first — full thresholds and qualifying conditions in our Terms & Conditions. Ad spend paid to Meta/Google is never covered.

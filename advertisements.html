The No-Loss Guarantee

Campaign Underperforms? We Refund Our Fee.

Run your campaign with us for 90 days. If it doesn't generate at least the minimum number of tracked leads for your spend level — leads we can show you directly from your own campaign data — we refund every dollar of our management fee for that period. No guessing, no "trust us," just the numbers from your own account.

Your ad spend goes straight to Meta or Google, so that part was never ours to give back — but the fee you pay us to manage it? If the campaign underperforms, you get it back.

Guarantee is based on tracked leads, not revenue or sales — see our Terms & Conditions for the exact thresholds and qualifying conditions. Ad spend paid to Meta/Google is never covered.

Common Questions

FAQ

Everything you want to know before you book a call.

Our management fee varies based on scope and platforms. Your ad spend goes directly to Meta or Google — we never mark it up. We'll give you a clear number on the strategy call, no surprises.
Most clients see inbound leads within the first 7–14 days. The first 30 days is a learning phase — results compound significantly in months 2 and 3. Paid ads aren't magic, but when built right they work reliably.
We recommend a minimum of $500/month in ad spend, paid directly to Meta or Google. Below that, the algorithm doesn't have enough data to optimize properly. For faster results, $1,000–$2,000/month is the sweet spot.
We specialize in Meta (Facebook & Instagram) and Google Ads. For most contractors, we start with Meta for lead volume and layer in Google for high-intent search traffic once the foundation is solid.
If we don't generate qualified leads within your first 30 days, you don't pay for month two. We're not interested in one-month transactions — we want long-term partners, and that only works if you're getting results.

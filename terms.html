4. Money-Back Guarantee

Clients enrolled in an advertising management plan (currently: the Premium plan) are eligible for the following guarantee, subject to the conditions below:

  • Guarantee period: 90 days from the date the ad campaign is first launched.
  • What's covered: Anaya Ventures' management fee only, for the guarantee period. Ad spend paid to Meta/Google is never covered or refundable.
  • Trigger condition — tracked clicks or tracked leads, not revenue: This guarantee is based on numbers Anaya Ventures can independently verify from its own campaign data — not on Client-reported sales or revenue, which we have no way to confirm. Two thresholds apply, based on Client's monthly ad spend:
    • $500/month minimum ad spend → 150 tracked clicks OR 15 tracked leads over 90 days
    • $1,000/month ad spend → 300 tracked clicks OR 30 tracked leads over 90 days
    • $2,000/month ad spend → 600 tracked clicks OR 60 tracked leads over 90 days
    • Spend levels between or above these tiers scale proportionally.

    Important — how the "or" works: reaching either threshold — clicks or leads, whichever happens first — satisfies the guarantee and voids Client's right to a refund for that period. Both thresholds do not need to be missed simultaneously for a refund to apply; conversely, hitting just one of the two means no refund is owed, even if the other threshold is not reached. In practice, the tracked-clicks threshold is expected to be reached through normal campaign delivery in nearly every case, since it depends primarily on ad spend and platform delivery rather than on Client's landing page, offer, or market conditions.

    A "tracked click" means a link click to Client's website, landing page, or contact form, as recorded in the ad platform's own reporting. A "tracked lead" means a form submission, phone call, or direct message generated through the ad campaign, as recorded in Anaya Ventures' ad platform reporting and/or call tracking system.

  • Conditions to qualify:
    • Client maintained the required minimum ad spend throughout the guarantee period
    • Client did not pause, cancel, or materially alter the campaign without Anaya Ventures' agreement
    • Client responded to leads/inquiries generated by the campaign in a timely manner
    • Client provided accurate business information, offers, and landing pages at the outset
    • Refund request is submitted in writing within 14 days of the guarantee period ending
  • Refund process: Anaya Ventures reviews campaign data against the tracked-click and tracked-lead thresholds above and confirms the result to Client in writing. Approved refunds are issued within 14 business days, covering management fees paid during the 90-day guarantee period only.
